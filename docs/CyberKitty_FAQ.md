# CyberKitty FAQ

Q: What is CyberVerse?
A: CyberVerse is an Android application for learning cybersecurity, exploring ethical hacking courses, discovering security hardware tools, and preparing for professional certifications.

Q: How do I talk to CyberKitty?
A: You can open CyberKitty by tapping the floating cat icon (FAB) on the Home, Shop, or Course screens, or by tapping "Talk to Kitty".

Q: Can I view my past chats with CyberKitty?
A: Yes! Tap the History icon in the top right header of the CyberKitty screen to view past sessions, switch between chats, or delete history.

Q: How do I start a new chat session?
A: Tap the `+` New Chat button in the CyberKitty header or in the history drawer.

Q: What courses are available?
A: CyberVerse offers free and paid courses covering Penetration Testing, CS Beginners, CyberSecurity Full Course, and Introduction to Information Security, plus ISC2 certification prep.

Q: How do I log in or create an account?
A: Tap on the Profile tab at the bottom navigation, then choose Login or Sign Up.
